Ongoing work

Projects & collaborations

Active projects and institutional partnerships the group is currently engaged in.

Chalcogenide Hybrid Nonlinear Photonics

Ongoing

C2N, Université Paris-Saclay (Dr. Laurent Vivien) · MIT (Prof. Juejun Hu)

International collaboration developing chalcogenide hybrid waveguides for nonlinear integrated photonic applications, connecting nanofabrication expertise at C2N with device design and testing across BSU and MIT.

FOTON-IA

2026 proposal

G8+ / Corporación Ruta N (Medellín) · Universidad de Medellín, UNAL, ITM, Universidad de Antioquia · BSU/MIT · ANITI (France)

Proposal to Ruta N's fourth joint G8+ call for district CTeI funding: a socio-technical platform for real-time monitoring of wastewater energy valorization in the Valle de Aburrá, combining hybrid electronic-photonic sensing with AI-driven early-warning analysis. CHIRP's role, alongside MIT, is providing PIC fabrication lab access and personnel for the sensor hardware; UNAL leads PIC sensor design/fabrication and bio-electrochemistry.

FUTUR-IC

Ongoing

MIT Initiative for Knowledge and Innovation in Manufacturing (IKIM) · Tyndall National Institute (Ireland) · LEAP Network

National initiative advancing resource-efficient, high-performance microchip manufacturing through package-level electronic-photonic integration, alongside value-chain innovation and workforce development for the semiconductor industry. Dr. Samuel Serna serves as Co-Principal Investigator and Technology Team Lead, directing BSU's contributions to electronic-photonic packaging technology as part of the LEAP network connecting industry, government, and academia in integrated photonics.

PIC Packaging with Ajinomoto

Ongoing

Ajinomoto Co., Inc. / Ajinomoto Fine-Techno Co., Inc.

Collaboration with Ajinomoto to build photonic integrated circuits, exploring Ajinomoto Build-up Film (ABF) — the amino-acid-derived interlayer insulation material best known for its role in high-performance CPU substrate packaging — for PIC packaging and substrate integration.

SiN PICs for Visible-Spectrum Spectrometry

Ongoing

Lightfinder (Cambridge, MA)

Collaboration with Lightfinder, an MIT-born startup developing chip-based spectrometers that combine integrated photonics with AI-driven signal processing, studying silicon nitride (SiN) photonic integrated circuits for spectrometry in the visible spectrum.

STAMPEDE

Ongoing

NSF FuSe-TG (Award #2235443) · Arizona State University · MIT · RIT · Clemson University

NSF-funded initiative developing scalable, low-cost nanomanufacturing for classical and quantum photonic integrated circuits, advancing extreme information-density PIC technology, anti-counterfeiting chip design, and STEM workforce training. Dr. Samuel Serna serves as Co-Principal Investigator alongside teams at ASU, MIT, RIT, and Clemson.

MIT Research Partnership

Ongoing since 2019

PMAT (Prof. Juejun Hu) · EMAT (Prof. Lionel Kimerling) · LEAP (Dr. Anuradha Agarwal)

Standing collaboration with MIT's Materials for Photonics and Materials for Electronics groups, including co-supervision of MIT graduate students and shared use of MIT.nano fabrication facilities for chip-to-chip and fiber-to-chip optical interconnects, nonlinear photonics, and integrated sensing.

Nonlinear Photonics with C2N

Ongoing since 2017

Centre de Nanosciences et de Nanotechnologies (C2N), Université Paris-Saclay

Continuing collaboration with the Silicon Photonics group at C2N (Dr. Laurent Vivien, Dr. Carlos Alonso-Ramos, Prof. Delphine Marris-Morini), spanning hybrid chalcogenide and crystalline-oxide nonlinear waveguides, sample exchange, and joint publications.

Nonlinear Optics with Institut d'Optique

Ongoing since 2020

Institut d'Optique Graduate School · LP2N (Prof. Nicolas Dubreuil)

Collaboration with Dr. Serna's PhD co-supervisor on nonlinear phenomena in integrated photonics for telecommunications, including co-supervised student internships and joint experimental work using BSU's heterodyne detection setup.

Student & Research Exchange with UNAL

Ongoing since 2019

Universidad Nacional de Colombia – Sede Medellín (Prof. Román Castañeda)

Institutional partnership co-advising graduate and undergraduate students between BSU and UNAL's photonics group, including student exchanges, joint weekly research seminars, and shared simulation and characterization work feeding into CHIRP's publications and conference presentations.
